How DomainScope works

DomainScope runs entirely in your browser. It queries public, key-less APIs and does not store your searches on any server.

A record (IPv4 address)

Maps a domain name to an IPv4 address. This is the most common way browsers reach a website.

AAAA record (IPv6 address)

Maps a domain name to an IPv6 address, the newer version of the Internet Protocol.

MX record (Mail exchange)

Tells email servers where to deliver mail for the domain, including a priority value.

TXT record (Text)

Holds arbitrary text, often used for SPF, DKIM and domain ownership verification.

NS record (Nameserver)

Lists the authoritative DNS servers for the domain.
